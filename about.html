About Hey, Stranger

Our Story

Hey, Stranger Coffee Collective was born out of a long-held dream. It started when our founder first experienced Melbourne's coffee culture back in 2012 and was hooked. Years later, in 2023, that dream became real: a small, family-run café on Cape Town's Long Street, built to be part of the creative melting pot of the CBD.

The collective is a genuine family effort. It's run day to day by a close-knit team, with everyone pitching in wherever they're needed — from admin to keeping the pastry counter stocked.

Our Mission

We believe a good café is a meeting place first and a coffee shop second. Our aim is simple: for people to walk in as strangers and leave as friends. Every cup is brewed with care and served with a smile, in a space designed to slow you down rather than rush you out.

Our Coffee

We serve exclusively Father Coffee beans. Father Coffee roasts around fifty-two different single origins a year, and we aim to bring as many of those as we can to our counter, so regulars always have something new to try. Our house Heirloom Blend has become a firm favourite with locals and visitors alike.

Our Space

Step inside and you'll find a terrazzo counter, simple wooden chairs, plenty of greenery and a tempting pastry case. It's a clean, minimalist interior, built for lingering over a flat white or getting a few hours of work done with free Wi-Fi.
