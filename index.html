Speciality coffee, minimally done

Hey, Stranger is a small, family-run café built around one simple idea: serve honest, exceptional coffee in a space that feels calm rather than rushed. We serve exclusively Father Coffee beans, roasted in small batches across dozens of single origins, so there is always something new to taste.

What we pour

From our house Heirloom Blend to rotating single-origin filters, cortados, cappuccinos and creamy matcha lattes, there's something to suit every taste. Pair it with a fresh pastry from our counter.
